### Hi, I'm Alexei

Computer Science at Princeton ('27), with minors in statistics, machine learning and optimization. I work on **mechanistic interpretability**: understanding what models actually represent and whether they use it. Before that I worked on perception and control: autonomous vehicles (Nuro, Princeton's PAVE team) and ML for space-telescope optics (NASA Ames).

**Research**

- [**Othello-GPT world-model emergence**](https://github.com/AlexeiKorolev/othello-world-model): a full-scale Othello-GPT reproduction, extended with calibrated causal interventions, a training-time trace (causal use saturates by epoch 4 while probes keep improving), probe drift across checkpoints, and a bilinear-MLP comparison.
- [**Speak-and-draw (senior thesis, in progress)**](https://github.com/AlexeiKorolev/speak-and-draw): one model that talks and draws at the same time, like a teacher at a whiteboard.
- [**Tokenizer data-mixture inference**](https://github.com/AlexeiKorolev/DeepSeek-Tokenizer-Attack): recovering training-data proportions from BPE merge rules, plus a randomized-merge defence.
- [**RL for deformable-mirror control**](https://github.com/AlexeiKorolev/NASA-RL-Wavefront-Control): learned wavefront correction for coronagraphs, with the NASA Ames Coronagraph Experiment team.
